Frequently asked questions

How do I renew my policy?

You can renew through the website, the chat assistant, WhatsApp, or by calling customer care with your policy number.

How do I register a motor claim?

Intimate the claim through any channel with your policy number, vehicle number and incident details. You will receive a claim reference number immediately.

Where can I find a cashless hospital or garage?

Ask the chat assistant or call customer care with your PIN code to get the nearest network hospital or garage.

How can I get a copy of my policy?

Request a policy copy over chat, WhatsApp or email. It will be sent to your registered email address.

How do I raise a grievance?

Contact customer care or write to the grievance desk. If unresolved, you may approach the regulator's grievance redressal mechanism.

Request a callback

Leave your details and a specialist will call you back. In the demo, this simulates an outbound callback task routed to an agent.
